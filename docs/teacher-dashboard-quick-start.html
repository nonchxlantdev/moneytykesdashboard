Teacher quick start

How the MoneyTykes Teacher Dashboard works

A plain-language guide to everyday classroom workflows—sign in, know your class, teach, reward, plan, and (for school leads) set up accounts.

Where things live

DashboardClass status, tasks, leaders, events
StudentsRoster, profiles, add student, attendance
LessonsCreate, organize, and present class materials
RewardsTemplates, award points, leaderboard
GamesRun live classroom money games
CalendarSchedule lessons, quizzes, reminders
AdminSchools, teachers, theme personalization

Six classroom workflows

1

Start your day

Open the Dashboard to see class health, upcoming events, and quick shortcuts before the first lesson.

  1. 1
    Sign in
    Login screen

    What you do

    Teachers sign in to reach their classroom workspace.

    Why it helps

    Each teacher sees their own class context and saved preferences.
  2. 2
    Land on Dashboard
    Dashboard

    What you do

    Read the chalkboard greeting, this month’s class status, recent tasks, and top students.

    Why it helps

    One glance shows progress on lessons, attendance, rewards, and tasks.
  3. 3
    Check the Events rail
    Right side of Dashboard

    What you do

    Scan upcoming calendar items, use Quick actions, open 501 Academy resources, or read the daily tip.

    Why it helps

    Shortcuts keep common actions one click away during a busy morning.
  4. 4
    Replay the How To tour anytime
    System → How To (sidebar)

    What you do

    Walk through highlighted parts of the real screen if you need a refresher.

    Why it helps

    New teachers get oriented without a separate training packet.

Done when: You know what needs attention today without digging through menus.

2

Know your class

Build and maintain the roster so every student can earn points, take attendance, and appear in reports.

  1. 1
    Open Students
    Classroom → Students

    What you do

    Browse the roster, search or filter, and open a student profile when needed.

    Why it helps

    The roster is the source of truth for who is in your class.
  2. 2
    Add a student
    Add Student wizard

    What you do

    Enter student details, guardian info, school/teacher assignment, then review and save.

    Why it helps

    Structured steps reduce missing contact or school information.
  3. 3
    Take attendance
    Students → Attendance

    What you do

    Mark present, late, or absent for the day (or class session).

    Why it helps

    Attendance feeds class status and helps you spot patterns early.

Done when: A complete, up-to-date class list ready for teaching and rewards.

3

Teach financial literacy

Create lessons, start them with the class, and use games when you want an active money lesson.

  1. 1
    Browse Lessons
    Classroom → Lessons

    What you do

    Find existing lessons, favorite the ones you reuse, and open create controls.

    Why it helps

    Your lesson library is the home for curriculum you teach repeatedly.
  2. 2
    Build a lesson
    Lesson Studio

    What you do

    Choose a type (class lesson plan, video, or presentation), fill in details, and save.

    Why it helps

    Different formats match how you actually teach—not one rigid template.
  3. 3
    Start / present
    Lessons library or presentation mode

    What you do

    Launch the lesson when class begins so students follow along on the board.

    Why it helps

    Teaching from the dashboard keeps materials and classroom flow together.
  4. 4
    Play a classroom game
    Classroom → Games

    What you do

    Pick a game (for example Money Moves Live), set teams, and run it on the board.

    Why it helps

    Games turn money concepts into lively practice, not only worksheets.

Done when: Students learn through planned lessons and classroom games.

4

Motivate with rewards

Define what students can earn, award points for good habits, and celebrate leaders.

  1. 1
    Create reward templates
    Classroom → Rewards

    What you do

    Name rewards, set point values, and keep a list of what students can work toward.

    Why it helps

    Templates make awarding points fast and consistent every day.
  2. 2
    Award points
    Rewards → Award points

    What you do

    Choose a student, pick a reward or amount, and confirm.

    Why it helps

    Immediate feedback helps students connect effort with recognition.
  3. 3
    Check the leaderboard
    Rewards + Dashboard

    What you do

    See who is ahead and celebrate progress with the class.

    Why it helps

    Friendly competition keeps financial habits visible and fun.

Done when: A clear points economy that reinforces the behaviors you care about.

5

Plan the week

Use Calendar (and soon Quizzes & Tests) to schedule lessons, quizzes, and reminders.

  1. 1
    Open Calendar
    Main → Calendar

    What you do

    View the month/week, create events, and edit or remove items when plans change.

    Why it helps

    Events also surface on the Dashboard rail so you do not miss them.
  2. 2
    Quizzes & Tests (coming soon)
    Classroom → Quizzes & Tests

    What you do

    Preview the upcoming quiz experience while the full feature is still rolling out.

    Why it helps

    Assessment will sit next to lessons and calendar once ready.
  3. 3
    My Day (coming soon)
    Main → My Day

    What you do

    A future home for personal to-dos and next-day planning.

    Why it helps

    Keeps teacher planning separate from the live class roster tools.

Done when: A shared picture of what is coming up for your class.

6

School setup (Admin)

School leads manage schools and teacher accounts, and personalize the look of the dashboard.

  1. 1
    Manage schools
    System → Admin → Schools tab

    What you do

    Add or edit participating schools and their contact details.

    Why it helps

    Students and teachers need a school to attach to during setup.
  2. 2
    Manage teachers
    System → Admin → Teachers tab

    What you do

    Create teacher accounts, assign a school, or reassign when staff move.

    Why it helps

    Each teacher account unlocks classroom tools for that school.
  3. 3
    Personalize the look
    Admin → Personalization

    What you do

    Pick a color theme using the thumbnail previews (saved on this device).

    Why it helps

    A familiar look helps teachers feel at home in the product.

Done when: Teachers are assigned to schools and the workspace matches your school brand.

Tip: In the live dashboard, teachers can also run System → How To for an on-screen tour that highlights the real interface. This handout is for training and onboarding conversations.
